# Workflow 5 — Counsellor Manager day-to-day

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Actors | Counsellor Manager, Counsellors, Education Manager (oversight) |
| Trigger | Daily, weekly, monthly cycles |
| Outcome | Team runs at target conversion with low SLA breach and managed quality |
| Languages | EN |

## Step-by-step

### 1. Morning standup screen (09:00)
Team dashboard shows: yesterday's outputs per counsellor; leads in/out; conversion trend (7d); SLA breaches (any lead untouched >24h); hot leads needing reassignment; counsellors at >130% load. Standup 15 min via integrated huddle.

### 2. Lead routing review (09:30)
Auto-router has assigned overnight leads. Manager overrides where needed: VIP/agent-priority to top-quartile counsellors; language-specific leads to language-aligned counsellors; capacity rebalancing.

### 3. QA review (10:00–12:00)
Sample 5–8 calls + 5–8 WhatsApp threads. QA tool: random sample weighted to ensure ≥5/counsellor/month; plays recording with transcript; 20-point rubric inline; notes per counsellor; auto-generated scorecard.

### 4. Escalation handling (12:00–14:00)
Queue separates by type: hostile parent threads; refund claims above counsellor limit; suspected fraud flags; Compliance grey-area cases. Triage: take over, coach the counsellor, escalate further. SLA: 30 min for hostile-parent.

### 5. 1:1s (14:00–16:00)
Four 1:1s per day at 30 min covers team of 20 over a week. Fixed agenda from SOP as checklist. Notes captured against counsellor's record.

### 6. Hiring / training / admin (16:00–17:30)
Hiring pipeline; interview scheduling; training prep/delivery. Weekly training session in team calendar.

### 7. End-of-day team thread (17:30)
Tomorrow's priorities; team-wide notices; one win or learning.
