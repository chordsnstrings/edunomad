# Parent FAQ — top 30

### 1. What does EduNomad actually do for my child?
We help your child apply to universities abroad and get the right visa. We shortlist universities matched to academics and budget; we package and submit applications; we prepare the visa file with a registered consultant; we arrange housing, banking, SIM, insurance and airport pickup; we check in for the first 90 days after arrival. Everything is visible to you in the app.

### 2. Can you guarantee my child will get a visa?
No one can. We make sure the file is complete, accurate, and consistent with what visa officers look for. Properly-prepared files have very different approval rates from improvised ones, but no guarantee is possible and we will not pretend otherwise.

### 3. What is the total cost?
Total = university tuition + GIC or financial-proof amount + visa fees + flights + first-year living costs + our service fee. We show a clear, itemised cost in the app once your child's destination and university are decided.

### 4. What is your refund policy?
Depends on the stage: profile-only — full refund minus admin fee; application packaged — partial; application submitted — smaller partial; offer received — smaller still; visa filed — per partner-uni and regulatory terms.

### 5. Who is the counsellor and what is their background?
Your child's counsellor is named in the app, with photo, languages, and tenure. All counsellors complete EduNomad's certified training and are reviewed monthly. You can request a different counsellor at any time, no reason required.

### 6. Why is the GIC for Canada CAD 22,895? Do we have to use GIC?
The CAD 22,895 amount was set by IRCC effective September 1, 2025. GIC is the strongly preferred way to show financial proof. Other proof is technically possible but the bar to convince the visa officer is much higher.

### 7. How is our money protected when we pay through your platform?
Payments go through regulated payment partners (bKash, Nagad, SSL, banks). We never ask for cash. We never ask for off-platform transfers. If anyone — including a counsellor — asks for money outside the platform, please report it to the manager immediately via the app.

### 8. Can my child work while studying?
In most destinations, yes — typically 20 hours per week during term, full-time during breaks. Please don't assume part-time work covers living costs; visa officers expect funds independent of work.

### 9. Will my child get permanent residency / citizenship after studying?
It depends on the country, programme, and post-study choices. We cannot promise PR. We can show you which programmes have post-study work visas and typical pathways.

### 10. How long does the whole process take?
Application to enrolment is typically 6–12 months. Visa decisions: Canada 8–16 weeks; UK 3–6 weeks; Australia 4–16 weeks; Malaysia 4–8 weeks.

### 11. What if my child's English is weak?
Options: MOI letters from school/college, Duolingo English Test (faster than IELTS), university foundation pathways with embedded English support.

### 12. What documents do I need to provide as a sponsor?
Typically: bank statements (6 months), tax returns or income proof, business documents if you own a business, sponsor declaration letter, your passport copy, proof of relationship to your child.

### 13. What if the visa is refused?
Your counsellor will call you and your child personally. We'll explain the reasons, outline reapplication or alternative pathways. Some of our service fee is refundable per policy.

### 14. Can I see what's happening with my child's application?
Yes — that's why we invited you to the app. You'll see every stage, every document status, every offer letter. Daily WhatsApp summary at 6pm. Message the counsellor directly anytime.

### 15. What if my child changes their mind about the destination?
Allowed, with implications. The application package usually needs redoing; some fees may need to be re-paid; the timeline shifts.

### 16. Will my child be looked after when they arrive?
Yes. Pre-Departure team confirms housing, opens a bank account, sets up SIM and insurance, books airport pickup. Check-ins at 30, 60, and 90 days. After that, your child joins our alumni network.

### 17. Who picks up my child from the airport?
A driver from our local transport partner. Driver name, phone, and meeting point are confirmed in the app and sent to you and your child by WhatsApp. Drivers vetted and licensed.

### 18. What insurance does my child have?
Health insurance is mandatory in most destinations and is part of the pre-departure package. Travel insurance also typical for the journey.

### 19. What about housing — is it safe?
Vetted Purpose-Built Student Accommodation operators and approved landlords near each campus. Photos, amenities, distance to campus, and monthly cost visible in the app before booking.

### 20. Can I talk to a manager if I have a concern?
Yes. "Talk to a manager" button. Counsellor Manager responds within 4 working hours. Serious concerns escalate to Education Manager.

### 21. Why is the counsellor asking us so many questions about funds?
Because visa officers will. Every visa application requires clear, documented proof of funds.

### 22. What if our family business doesn't have formal documentation?
Common situation. We help structure what you do have — bank statements, trade licences, tax records, asset valuations, sponsor declarations.

### 23. Will the university we choose actually accept my child?
We shortlist with a mix of reach, match, and safe universities. The match and safe options are likely. We never promise a specific university.

### 24. What is the difference between a conditional and unconditional offer?
Conditional means the university wants to accept IF certain conditions are met — typically a final transcript, an English test score, or a deposit. Unconditional means all conditions satisfied.

### 25. When do we pay tuition?
Typically a deposit (one semester) after the offer is accepted, before the visa is filed. Final tuition before enrolment.

### 26. Can my child come home if they don't like it there?
Of course. Visas allow travel home. Some students transfer to a different university, defer, or withdraw. We support each path.

### 27. What happens if my child gets sick or has an emergency abroad?
Health insurance covers medical needs. For non-medical emergencies, the Pre-Departure Coordinator and a 24/7 emergency contact remain reachable through the first 90 days.

### 28. What if I disagree with my spouse about my child's plans?
The counsellor's job is to lay out facts, not take sides. We can schedule a family call where both parents and the student are on the line.

### 29. What is your team's qualification to advise on visas?
Visa file sign-off is performed by a registered consultant: RCIC / CICC for Canada, MARA for Australia, OISC for paid UK advice. Registration number appears on the signed file.

### 30. What if I'm not happy with the service?
Use the "Talk to a manager" button. Counsellor Manager investigates every complaint, responds within 4 working hours, resolves within 5 business days. Refunds, where the policy applies, are processed by Finance.
